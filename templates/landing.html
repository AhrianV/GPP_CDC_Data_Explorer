About the project

Frequently asked questions

Who created this app?

This educational app was created by students from CodeWizardsHQ to make CDC health data easier to explore and understand.

Where does the data come from?

The dashboards use publicly available data from the CDC. Update timing depends on the source dataset and the CDC.

From charts to understanding

Get help interpreting what you see.

Request an AI analysis of a displayed graph for a plain-language explanation of key trends, notable changes, and possible context.

The analysis uses the graph title and a sample of its data. Use it as a starting point for exploration, and check conclusions against the chart and original source.

How to get started

  1. Create an account or sign in to open your dashboard.
  2. Choose diseases, vaccinations, or nutrition.
  3. Explore the charts and compare the available values.
  4. Use the graph's AI analysis option to help interpret its patterns.

About the data

A little context for your exploration.

Where does the data come from?

Disease and vaccination charts use CDC public datasets. Nutrition information comes from USDA FoodData Central when available, with Open Food Facts as an alternative source.

Does every chart show current data?

The available dates depend on the source dataset, and results may be cached. Check the dates and labels on each chart; historical data does not necessarily describe current conditions.

What is this project designed for?

CDC Data Explorer is a student project for exploring public health data and practicing data interpretation. Its charts and AI explanations support learning, rather than diagnosis or treatment planning.
