About CDC Data Explorer

CDC Data Explorer is a student-built application created as part of the CodeWizardsHQ Group Project Program. Students worked together as a development team to build an app for exploring vaccination coverage, reported disease cases, and food-product nutrition.

Explore charts, save your favorites, or upload a dataset for summaries and AI-assisted analysis. The project brings together the team's frontend and full stack development skills in one shared application.
